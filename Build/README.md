# Johnny
## Adds Johnny from the Guilty Gear series as a playable character!

![Alt Text](https://imgur.com/jJykEXm.png "Johnny Loadout Display")

Any feedback can be submitted to the discord; https://discord.gg/xpP6AgxWYG

![Alt Text](https://imgur.com/2pPE2qf.png "Johnny Character Icon")

### Passive : Turn Up
![Alt Text](https://imgur.com/kjkLdWZ.png "Passive Icon")

Getting hit below 60% max health will fill you with the rage of Oryx the Mad God.

### Primary : Slash
![Alt Text](https://imgur.com/PWdhJeI.png "Primary Icon")

Swings his katana less fast than below

### Secondary : Mist Finer
![Alt Text](https://imgur.com/DTaUS0m.png "Secondary Icon")

Johnny channels his inner cool guy and swings his katana faster than light making a god damn gunshot basically.

### Utility : Vault
![Alt Text](https://imgur.com/4CA4EWJ.png "Utility Icon")

Flip forward cause you're a fancy bastard and you wanna look like a cool guy

### Mist Finer Utility : Mist Finer Teleport
![Alt Text](https://imgur.com/D0iyJqO.png "Mist Finer Dash Icon")

Instantly teleport through space and time in the direction you hold.

### Special : Deal
![Alt Text](https://imgur.com/kjkLdWZ.png "Special Icon")
